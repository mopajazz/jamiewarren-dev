A researcher keeps monarchs indoors on a light cycle shifted six hours late, then releases them under a clear sky. Predict their error before revealing:

Reveal

They fly roughly 90° off course — six hours of sun movement, uncorrected. This clock-shift signature is precisely how researchers proved the sun compass is time-compensated, and your second row is the same effect produced a different way.

Boundaries and simplifications

  • Sun azimuth moves linearly east to west at 15°/hour (07:00–19:00). Real azimuth motion varies with latitude, season, and hour.
  • "Clock disabled" freezes the internal clock at dawn, isolating time-compensation. Real manipulations — antennae painted black, clock-shifting, clock-gene disruption — produce related but messier misorientation.
  • Real migrants scatter around the mean bearing; flight here is deterministic so runs compare cleanly. Wind, thermals, and refueling are out of scope.
  • The magnetic compass is light-dependent (UV-A/blue), which still penetrates overcast — that is why the backup works under clouds.
